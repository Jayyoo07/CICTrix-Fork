Rules

None
“No positions assigned” in Body S, #94A3B8. Never an empty cell.
Exactly one
The title itself as a brand-tint chip. No control: there is nothing to expand.
Two or more
A chip reading “n positions” with a caret, collapsed by default. The titles open beneath it, each on a 2px #C8D1FF left rule.
Colour
Brand tint only (#EEF0FF on #191FA8). A position is a category, not a status (§10), so never green, orange or red — and never link blue, since it does not navigate.
Control
A real <button> carrying aria-expanded, focus ring 2px #363EE8 at 2px offset (§13). The caret rotates 90° in 150ms, and not at all under prefers-reduced-motion.
Row height
Collapsed rows stay within the 48–56px of §9.5 whatever the count. Expansion is per row and does not persist.
Truncation
A single chip caps at 220px and ellipsises; the expanded list never truncates (§9.5: no truncating an identifier without recourse).
